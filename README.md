# The Whispering Body — First-Aid Box

**A companion app for the book *The Whispering Body: An Alternate Explanation and Storyteller's Guide to Homeopathy* by Dr. Sohil Momin.**

🔗 **Open the app:** https://drsamonline.github.io/whispering-body-first-aid/

---

## What it is

A small, offline home first-aid companion built from Chapter Seven of the book.
It holds **30 commonly used homeopathic first-aid remedies** and helps you match
one to the whole picture of a symptom.

- **Find** — describe the problem in plain language and see the matching remedy.
- **The Box** — all 30 remedies, grouped, with search.
- **Emergency** — the signs that mean "get medical help now", with a call button.
- **Guide** — how to dose, how a remedy is made (dilution to globules), how to
  carry and handle the box, and how to use it.
- **Languages** — English, हिन्दी and ગુજરાતી.

## Install it on your phone

Open the link above in your phone's browser, then use **Add to Home screen**
(Share ▸ Add to Home Screen on iOS; the ⋮ menu ▸ Add to Home screen on Android).
It then runs like an app and works without an internet connection.

## Safety

This app gives general information about homeopathic first-aid practice. It is
**not** a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified clinician, and it
is never a reason to delay conventional medical care. In an emergency, call for
help first (112 / 108 in India).
