## What this app is

**The Whispering Body** is a first-aid reference — what to do in the first minutes of a common injury or emergency, and what not to do.

It is a **reference and reading tool**. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, prescribe, treat or cure any condition.

## Not medical advice

Every rubric, remedy, keynote, relationship or passage shown here is **quoted from a historical text**, not a recommendation. A remedy suggested by a repertory is a hypothesis for a registered practitioner to test against the individual patient — it is not a prescription, and it is not advice to you.

No doctor–patient relationship arises from using this app.

**This app does not replace emergency care.** First aid is what you do while help is coming, not instead of it. If someone is unconscious, not breathing, bleeding heavily, or has had a fall, burn or poisoning, call emergency services first and follow their instructions.

## Sources and their limits

The guidance is compiled from standard first-aid practice and the app's own editorial work. It is not a reproduction of any single copyrighted manual.

Where classical authors disagree, the disagreement is preserved. It is not resolved here. Homeopathic literature reflects the understanding of its own time and is not a statement of modern clinical evidence.

First-aid guidance changes as evidence changes, and it differs by country. Where this app and your local emergency service disagree, follow the emergency service. If a step here is out of date, report it — that is the most useful thing a reader can do.

## If you are unwell

Consult a registered medical practitioner. Do not start, stop or delay treatment on the basis of anything shown here.

**In an emergency, call 108 (India) or your local emergency number.**

## Responsibility

Clinical decisions remain with the qualified practitioner treating the patient. This app records and displays what its sources wrote; it does not decide anything.

If a passage is misquoted, misattributed or mistranscribed, write to sohilmomin2000@gmail.com and it will be corrected.
