# Whispering Body — First Aid companion

**PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0**

A small offline home first-aid companion built from Chapter Seven of the book. It holds **30 commonly
used homeopathic first-aid remedies** and helps match one remedy to the whole picture of a symptom —
the "listen, then match" method the book teaches.

Open it: <https://drsamonline.github.io/whispering-body-first-aid/play/>

## Install it on your phone

1. Open the link above in your phone's browser.
2. **Add to Home screen** — *Share ▸ Add to Home Screen* on iOS, or the **⋮** menu ▸ *Add to Home
   screen* on Android.
3. It now opens like an app and works offline.

## What is in it

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **Find** | pick the problem in plain language — "a burn or a scald", "food poisoning" — and see the matching remedy |
| 🧰 **The Box** | all 30 remedies, grouped, with search; tap one for what it is for and its keynote |
| ⚠️ **Emergency** | the signs that mean *get medical help now*, on a red panel with a one-tap call button |
| 📖 **Guide** | how to dose · how a remedy is made (mother tincture → 1C → 30C → globules) · how to carry and handle the box |
| 🌐 **Languages** | English · हिन्दी · ગુજરાતી — the whole interface and content |
| 📴 **Offline** | installs to the home screen and runs without a connection |

## Safety — read this before using it

This app gives **general information** about homeopathic first-aid practice. It is **not** a
substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified clinician, and it is never a reason to delay
conventional medical care.

**In an emergency, seek conventional medical help first.** In India, call **112** or **108**.

The app's Emergency panel exists for exactly this: it lists the signs that mean *now*, and dials with
one tap.
